Training structure

Built to make
you better.

Every session includes age- and ability-appropriate running, drills, strength, speed development and instruction. Groups can fit different experience levels. The goal is stronger, smarter, more confident runners.

EXPERIENCE LEVELS

All levels.

You don't have to be the fastest. You don't have to be the most experienced. Training is matched to age and ability, and groups can accommodate different experience levels.

What every athlete needs is the willingness to run, work and get better.

PHILOSOPHY

Right kids,
not most kids.

This is a running club with a purpose, not a babysitting program. Athletes train in a positive, structured environment and learn distance-running fundamentals while building consistency, teamwork, discipline and confidence.

The club is not trying to build the biggest club. It is trying to build the right one.

What athletes

SCHOOLS

Different jerseys.
Same miles.

Last year the club had athletes from 13 different schools, among them Monroe High School, Monroe Middle School, Jefferson, Ida, Trinity Lutheran, Carlson, Summerfield and St. Mary Catholic Central (SMCC). The club grew from 16 athletes in 2025 to 53 in 2026.

How we measure success

Stack the days.
Stack the miles.

Success means seeing every runner improve from where they start at the beginning of summer to where they finish, and watching that improvement continue when they return to compete for their school.
