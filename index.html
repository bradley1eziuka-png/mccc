Who this is for

All runners.
All schools.
All levels.

The Monroe Cross Country Club is open to runners entering 5th through 12th grade from all schools, including homeschool athletes. Beginners through experienced competitors are welcome.

Athletes train in a positive, structured environment. They learn the fundamentals of distance running while they build consistency, teamwork, discipline and confidence.

This is a running club with a purpose. Not a babysitting program.

You don’t have to be fast.

You don’t have to be experienced.

But you do have to be willing to

Run.

Work.

Get better.

The philosophy

We want the right kids, not the most kids.

You don’t have to be the fastest. You don’t have to be the most experienced. But you need to want to be here.

This isn’t babysitting or just logging miles. Athletes learn discipline, consistency, goal setting, how to handle discomfort, and what it means to work toward something they want.

We’re not trying to build the biggest club.

We’re trying to build the right one.
